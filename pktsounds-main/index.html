Frequently Asked Questions

What is your rental process?+

Our rental process is simple: 1) Contact us with your event details, 2) We provide a customized quote, 3) Confirm booking with a deposit, 4) We deliver and set up equipment, 5) Enjoy your event!

Do you offer delivery and setup services?+

Yes, we offer professional delivery, setup, and teardown services. Our team ensures all equipment is properly installed and functioning before your event begins.

What happens if equipment fails during my event?+

We provide backup equipment for critical components and have technicians on standby for emergencies. Your event's success is our priority.

Can I rent equipment for multiple days?+

Absolutely! We offer flexible rental periods from single-day to multi-week rentals. Contact us for long-term rental discounts.

Do you require a security deposit?+

Yes, we require a refundable security deposit to cover any potential damages. The amount varies based on the equipment rented.
